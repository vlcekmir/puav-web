Principles of Sustainable Filmmaking

Thirteen Key Points of sustainable filmmaking

The Principles of Sustainable Filmmaking were created by experts and organisations from across the Czech audiovisual industry as a voluntary, practical framework for reducing the environmental impact of filmmaking and strengthening the competitiveness of the Czech audiovisual industry.

Practical tools

We create, test and share practical tools

We listen to producers, creators and other audiovisual professionals and turn their experience into solutions that we test together and make available to the whole industry.

Ready to use

Tools and standards available right now.

Database

Grid connection map and service database

Productions often struggle to find grid connections and reliable services near their locations. With the map, professionals can find suitable local solutions faster and save time when preparing a project. Guide to temporary grid connection (in Czech) · Add a location to the map

Standard

Principles of Sustainable Filmmaking

Productions lacked a common framework for sustainable filmmaking suited to Czech conditions. The Principles set out thirteen Key Points and nine Recommended points for the production of audiovisual works, giving producers and crews clear guidance on what to include in pre-production, production and the final evaluation of a project. View the Principles

Reporting

Unified reporting on the Principles

Television and independent productions used to report on the Principles in different ways. Since spring 2025, a unified methodology and a common structure for the final report have been available. This makes reporting easier for productions and allows results to be compared, evaluated and used for further improvement.

Education

Lifelong learning courses at FAMU

Until now, professionals lacked accessible training linking sustainability with everyday filmmaking practice. FAMU now offers free courses on film production, data and reporting, and environmental communication. Participants gain practical knowledge for their projects and an internationally recognised micro-credential. More information (in Czech)

Energy in testing

EcoBox: measuring energy use on set

Productions often lack accurate data on the electricity consumption of individual departments. EcoBox, developed by Planet A Collective and Kluci Base, allows live monitoring of consumption by department directly on set. It helps the production team size the generator correctly, assess the option of a grid connection and cut unnecessary consumption. Contact

Social sustainability collecting input

Safety on set

The producer’s responsibility for safety covers everyone present on set with their knowledge — not only their own employees. The documents in preparation summarise what the law requires in this area and what good practice looks like in individual areas of production. They will clarify what producers are responsible for and give crews support when they point out a risk. Share your experience from practice: Write to us
