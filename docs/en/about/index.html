Who we are

We connect people, knowledge and practice.

We are an open industry platform connecting producers, directors, broadcasters, professional associations, film schools and sustainability experts. We share proven know-how and create practical tools, databases and common standards that make sustainable decisions easier – from developing an idea through filming to distribution. Our goal is for sustainability to become a clear and natural part of professional audiovisual practice.

Platform Manager

Mgr. BcA. Miroslav Vlček, Ph.D. has been the manager of the Czech Platform for Sustainable Audiovisual Production since January 2025, when the position was established. He coordinates the work of the Platform and its working groups, which look for ways to reduce the environmental impact of film and television production in the Czech context. He is responsible for the Principles of Sustainable Filmmaking, harmonising reporting among members and developing practical tools for crews and screenwriters. He gives talks on behalf of PUAV, moderates expert discussions and liaises with institutions. He is also a member of the international network ASPA (Audiovisual Sustainability Professionals Association).

He studied Radio and Television Dramaturgy and Screenwriting at the Theatre Faculty of the Janáček Academy of Music and Performing Arts in Brno, and Media Studies and Journalism at Masaryk University, where he also earned his doctorate in General Theory and History of Art and Culture at the Department of Film and Audiovisual Culture, with a dissertation in production studies. He is an assistant professor at the Department of Sociology, Faculty of Arts, University of Ostrava, where he also guarantees the Journalism study programme. His research focuses on media industries from the perspective of sustainability in a broader sense – not only environmental but also social – in connection with the climate crisis, the rise of artificial intelligence and the precarisation of work.

Memorandum · 27 September 2024

Key players of the Czech audiovisual industry signed a Memorandum for a sustainable audiovisual industry

At the Serial Killer international festival in Brno, six key players of the Czech audiovisual industry signed the Memorandum on the Establishment of the Czech Platform for Sustainable Audiovisual Production: APA, ARAS, Czech Television, FAMU, FTV Prima and TV Nova.

What matters is not the signing itself. What matters is that organisations that normally compete on the market sat down together and agreed that sustainability should not be a matter of competition. Standards, data and tools only make sense if they apply to the whole industry.

The signatories committed to harmonising procedures for reducing the impact of production, sharing experience, supporting education and creating a database of responsible suppliers. The Platform started its work in January 2025 and has since grown to 23 members.
